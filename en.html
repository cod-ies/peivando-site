Multilingual is not an add-on. It is the reason.

Most agencies translate the text and call it multilingual. A site in Dari or Farsi runs right to left — layout, numbers, forms, navigation. We build that properly, because we work in these languages ourselves.

FAQ

What clients ask before we start.

What does a website from Peivando cost?

Projects start at US$100. What that covers depends on scope — a single landing page costs less than a multilingual business site. In the free first conversation you get a real number, not a range.

How long until my website is online?

It depends how quickly text and images are ready. We agree the timeline together in the first conversation, before you commit — you get a date, not an estimate.

Can I change the content myself later?

Yes. We show you how to edit text and images. If you would rather hand that off, we maintain it for you, one-off or ongoing.

Do you build sites in Dari or Farsi?

Yes, and properly: right-to-left layout, real Persian typography, and a language switch that flips forms and navigation too. We work in Dari and Farsi ourselves — it is not translation bolted on afterwards.

Do I own the website afterwards?

Yes. You get the files and the domain is in your name. No page-builder subscription you cannot leave.

Do you only work with clients in Munich?

Munich is our base and where meeting in person is normal. Everything else works remotely, anywhere — in German, English, Dari or Farsi.
